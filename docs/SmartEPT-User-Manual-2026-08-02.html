SmartEPT — User Manual

A simple, step-by-step guide anyone can follow — for the Owner/Admin who sets it up, the Manager who watches their team, and the Employee whose PC runs the agent. No technical knowledge needed.
02 Aug 2026 For: Owner/Admin · Manager · Employee Covers: Web Console + Desktop Agent Screenshots: add where marked
What is SmartEPT? SmartEPT is a system that shows you how work time is really being spent. It quietly records attendance, active vs idle time, which apps and websites are used, breaks, meetings and productivity — then turns all of it into a live dashboard, simple scores and ready-to-print reports. There are two pieces: a web console (opened in a browser, where owners and managers work) and a desktop agent (a small visible app installed on each employee's PC). SmartEPT is always visible and consent-based — employees can see it running; there is no hidden mode.

1. The basics — signing in & finding your way

Opening the console

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome or Edge) and go to your SmartEPT web address (your admin will give it to you, e.g. https://yourcompany.smartept…).
  2. Type your email and password and click Sign in.
  3. The first time you sign in you will be asked to set a new password. Choose one only you know, then you land on your home screen.
[Screenshot]The SmartEPT sign-in screen
Good to know: Forgot your password? Ask your admin to reset it for you — they can do it in seconds from the Users screen.

Finding your way around

The console has a menu on the left and your content on the right. What you see in the menu depends on your role — an employee sees only their own information, a manager sees their team, an admin sees everything. Every screen has a small ⓘ Help button that explains that screen in plain language.

3. Owner / Admin guide Owner / Admin

This is the biggest section — it walks through setting up and running SmartEPT, screen by screen, roughly in the order you would use them.

3.1 · Dashboard — your live board

What it's for: a real-time picture of who is working right now — Active, Idle, on a Break, in a Meeting, or Offline — plus totals for the day and the health of each PC's agent.

What you can do: click any tile (e.g. "On Break") to see exactly who is in that state; spot a PC whose agent stopped reporting.

[Screenshot]The live dashboard with status tiles and the team list
Good to know: The dashboard is the single truth — the same numbers appear in the reports, so what you see live and what you print always agree.

3.2 · Set up your company (Org)

What it's for: describe your business structure so everything else lines up — Branches (offices), Departments, Teams, Designations (job titles) and Shifts (working hours).

  1. Open Org from the menu.
  2. Add your Branches first, then Departments, then Teams.
  3. Add your Shifts — set the start & end time and the allowed break minutes. This is what "late" and "productivity" are measured against, so set it correctly.
[Screenshot]The Org screen showing branches, departments, teams and shifts
Good to know: If you skip the shift start time, "late minutes" cannot be calculated — always give each employee a shift.

3.3 · Add your employees

What it's for: create the people SmartEPT will track and assign them to a branch/department/team/shift.

  1. Open Employees → Add employee, fill in name, email and their branch/team/shift.
  2. Have a lot of people? Use Bulk import to add them all from a spreadsheet in one go.
  3. When someone leaves, use Relieve — SmartEPT stops tracking them and keeps a tidy archive of their records (which you can download or restore later).
[Screenshot]The Add-Employee form and the Employees list

3.4 · Choose what is tracked (Policies & Rules)

What it's for: you decide what SmartEPT records and enforces. There are separate, simple policies for each area:

PolicyControls
MonitoringWhether tracking is on, and how sensitive "idle" is.
ScreenshotsWhether screenshots are taken, and how often.
Webcam presenceWhether the app checks a person is at their desk (numbers only — see the Employee section).
Applications / WebsitesWhich apps & sites count as productive, and which are blocked.
Breaks / AttendanceAllowed break time; how late and check-out are decided.
USB / Network / VPNOptional restrictions on removable drives, networks and VPNs.

How assignment works (in plain words): you can set a rule for the whole company, or a specific branch, department, team, person or even one PC. The most specific rule always wins. If you set nothing, nothing is tracked — SmartEPT is "off by default" and safe.

[Screenshot]The Policies screen with the assignment options
Note: Nothing sensitive (screenshots, camera) is ever captured unless you switch that policy on and the employee has given consent on their agent.

3.5 · Attendance

What it's for: the daily attendance sheet — who came in, when, late minutes, half-days, absences — built automatically from logins and (if used) door punches.

  • Review the day's sheet; a mid-day step out for lunch is never mistaken for going home.
  • Need to correct something? Use the manual adjust (HR regularization) — SmartEPT keeps a record of the change.
  • Add Holidays so they are not counted as absences.
  • Export the attendance register for payroll.
[Screenshot]The attendance sheet with late/half-day/absent markers

3.6 · Biometric & Gate-to-PC

What it's for: connect your door biometric device so punches flow into SmartEPT, and (optionally) turn on Gate-to-PC — our signature feature.

Gate-to-PC in one line: the employee's PC stays locked for work until they physically punch in at the door. No punch, no working desktop — which stops "buddy punching" and proxy attendance from day one.
  1. Open Biometric, add your device, and map each device user to a SmartEPT employee.
  2. Turn the Gate On, Off (for a trial), or leave it Auto (follows your device setup).
  3. If the door hardware ever fails, an admin can grant an emergency override so work isn't blocked — every override is recorded.
[Screenshot]The Biometric screen and the Gate-to-PC switch

3.7 · Meetings, Reports & Exports

Meetings: schedule meetings, see who joined, and let SmartEPT close them on time automatically.

Reports: the heart of the console — Productivity (a clear 0–100 score), Compliance, Breaks, Monthly summary, and a per-employee timeline. Every report can be exported to a spreadsheet for payroll or records.

[Screenshot]A productivity report with scores and the Export button
Good to know: A "transparent" version of the productivity report shows exactly how each score was calculated — helpful when discussing performance with a team member.

3.8 · Screenshots, Webcam & Violations (evidence)

What it's for: when your policies allow it, review captured screenshots, webcam-presence records and a feed of policy violations (e.g. a blocked app opened). Each violation shows only its own evidence, so you're never sifting through unrelated pictures.

[Screenshot]The Violations feed with the per-event evidence view

3.9 · Users, Roles & Licence

Users & Roles: add manager/admin logins and tick exactly what each role can do. Licence: install or check your SmartEPT licence key (or import your offline licence file for an on-premises install).

[Screenshot]The Roles permission matrix and the Licence screen

3.10 · Settings & Ops (housekeeping)

What it's for: the behind-the-scenes settings — company email (SMTP) with a Send test email button, your business WhatsApp number, storage & retention, nightly backups, and Help → Troubleshooting (a live self-check that turns red if something needs attention).

4. Manager / Team-Leader guide Manager / Team Leader

As a manager you see your own team only — SmartEPT automatically limits you to the people who report to you (and the teams under your team leads). You don't see the rest of the company.

4.1 · Your live dashboard

Open the Dashboard to see, right now, who on your team is Active, Idle, on a Break or in a Meeting. Click a tile to get the filtered list.

[Screenshot]A manager's team dashboard

4.2 · Attendance & productivity for your team

  • Attendance — who came in, late minutes, breaks.
  • Productivity report — each person's score for the day/range; open the transparent view to see how it was built.
  • Timelines — a minute-by-minute picture of one person's day.
  • Breaks review — approve or note breaks that ran long.
  • Exports — download your team's numbers to a spreadsheet.
[Screenshot]A team productivity report

4.3 · Meetings

Schedule a meeting for your team, share it, and see who actually joined. If you have the permission, you can also end a meeting for everyone.

Good to know: If a menu item or button is missing, it simply means your role doesn't have that permission — ask your admin if you need it.

5. Employee guide — the desktop agent Employee

The agent is a small app on your work PC. It is always visible — you can see it running — and it only records what your company's policy allows, after you agree.

5.1 · Installing & first sign-in

  1. Run the installer your admin gives you (Windows, Mac or Linux) and open SmartEPT Agent.
  2. Sign in with your work email and password (same as the console). The window registers your PC as your device.
  3. Read the consent screen — it tells you plainly what will and won't be recorded — and accept to begin. Tracking will not start until you do.
[Screenshot]The agent sign-in and consent screens
Your privacy: There is no hidden mode. The webcam feature (if your company uses it) only reports simple numbers like "someone is at the desk" and a brightness reading — no photo or video is recorded or sent unless a photo policy is explicitly turned on. Screenshots are only taken if your company's policy enables them, and you can see the app is running.

5.2 · The gate wall (if your office uses Gate-to-PC)

If your company uses Gate-to-PC, the agent shows a friendly lock screen until you punch in at the door. Once your punch reaches SmartEPT (within a few minutes), your PC unlocks for work. If you punch out mid-day, the PC gently locks and a break starts automatically until you punch back in.

[Screenshot]The agent gate/lock screen asking for a door punch

5.3 · Breaks & meetings

Use the agent buttons to start a Tea, Lunch or Other break, or to mark a Meeting. You can only be in one at a time — end your current break before starting another (the app guides you).

[Screenshot]The agent break/meeting buttons and live status

5.4 · Working offline

No internet for a while? The agent keeps working and saves everything locally, then sends it automatically when the connection returns. A small counter shows how much is waiting to sync — you don't need to do anything.

5.5 · Your self-service portal

You can sign in to the web console too, where you'll see only your own information — your attendance, your productivity, your breaks — and you can export your own report. You cannot see anyone else's data.

[Screenshot]The employee self-service view

7. If something looks wrong

A PC's agent stopped reporting. Check the device is on and online; the Dashboard's device-health and Help → Troubleshooting will point to the cause.

Times or nightly reports look stale. This usually means the background scheduler on the server isn't running — the Troubleshooting Background scheduler tile turns red when this happens. Share that with your admin/IT.

Someone can't see a screen or button. Their role doesn't have that permission — an admin can grant it in Users & Roles.

Late minutes are blank. The employee has no shift start time assigned — set a shift in Org.

Note: Every SmartEPT product has an in-app Help → Troubleshooting page written in plain language, so most questions can be answered on the spot without calling anyone.
